How it works

Four steps, all in Google Docs.

  1. Write your notes.
    After a lesson, add your notes, the new words and any homework to your daily Google Doc.
  2. Set the row to Prepare email.
    That is the only button you press.
  3. Read the draft.
    It appears in the same Doc. Change anything you like.
  4. It goes to your student.
    The lesson is also added to that student's own Doc, so you keep a clean record.

Why teachers like it

It lives in the Google Docs you already use. Nothing to install and no new place to check.

You always see the e-mail before it goes out, and everything stays in your own Google Drive.
