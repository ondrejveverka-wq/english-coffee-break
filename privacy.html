Privacy policy

What this website does with your data.

Last updated 12 October 2026.

Who runs this site

English Coffee Break is run by Ondřej Veverka, an English teacher. Contact: onrejveverka@gmail.com.

What I collect

This site has no accounts, no forms, no analytics and no advertising. I do not set cookies. I do not collect your name or e-mail address through the site.

If you write to me, for example to ask for early access to the follow-up tool, I receive your e-mail address and your message. I use them only to reply, and I do not share them.

What happens when you visit

  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when you load a page.
  • Fonts. The text uses fonts loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google to fetch them.
  • Etsy. Lesson links and the shop button open Etsy, which has its own privacy policy. Buying, payment and the delivery of paid lessons are handled entirely by Etsy.
  • Free lessons. The free PDFs are plain downloads from this site. I do not ask for or store anything when you download them.

The follow-up tool

The follow-up tool is in early access and is not offered through this site yet. When it opens to other teachers, it will have its own privacy policy that explains exactly which parts of your Google account it uses and why, before you connect anything.

Your rights

You can ask me at any time what I hold about you, and ask me to delete it. Write to onrejveverka@gmail.com.
